VISION

A world where no store flies blind again.

The internet made digital retail measurable to the millisecond. Physical retail has been flying blind since glass windows were invented. Our vision is the parity: every shopfront as measurable as its own P&L — and intelligence that grows in the same direction as privacy, never against it.

MISSION

Turn the camera you own into an honest witness.

We measure the street before the door and the floor past it — on-device, from the cameras a store already owns. Then we ship the answer as a Monday Brief: a verdict, the evidence, and three actions worth taking. No dashboards to babysit. No identity captured. Just the number and the move.
